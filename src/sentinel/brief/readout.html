A personal investment research system for a UK retail investor. It aggregates market data, scores candidates with explicit reasoning, and enforces a risk layer no signal can override. Its north-star requirement is this page: report honestly when the benchmark is winning.

The limits no signal can override

The risk engine imports nothing from the analysis or LLM layers, and never reads conviction or composite score. It cannot be argued with by a persuasive memo.

Ideas

Everything generated, including what was refused

Rejected ideas are kept. Deleting them would make the rejection rate — the number that tells you whether the synthesis prompts are drifting — unmeasurable.

Evals

Written to be able to return a negative verdict

A number without its sample size is not a result, so every rate here carries its interval. These are the gates that can demote a module, and they are pre-committed in the spec so they cannot be rationalised away after the fact.

Data health

A signal generated from bad data is a Sev-1

So a critical issue means the ticker was not scored at all — not scored and flagged. Severity wears the reserved status palette rather than series colours.
